Frequently asked questions

Before you book.

What does Seamrite Designs offer?

Seamrite Designs offers made to measure clothing, custom dresses, bridal and formal wear, uniforms, swimwear, carnival costumes, alterations, original artwork and wearable art.

Where is Seamrite Designs based?

Seamrite Designs is a custom fashion and art studio based in Trinidad and Tobago, serving clients in South Trinidad and beyond.

How much does custom clothing cost?

Starting prices are listed on the pricing section. Your final quote depends on the design, fabric, measurements, details and time required.

What happens at an appointment?

An appointment helps us understand your design, measurements, fabric preferences, timeline and budget before work begins.

Are deposits refundable?

Down payments are required for customised clothing. Deposits are non refundable once work has started.

How do I book?

Use the appointment form to request a time for your clothing, alteration, costume or artwork request. Seamrite Designs will confirm the appointment by WhatsApp.
